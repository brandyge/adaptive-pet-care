###  Respite for Senior & Medical-Needs Pets in San Diego
Being the primary caregiver for a senior or disabled pet is an act of profound love—but it can also be isolating. I know the weight of the 'what ifs,' the missed dinners, and the anxiety of leaving, even for just a few hours.

I founded Sunset Paws to provide reliable breathing room for dedicated pet parents. By utilizing a small workspace in your home, I provide a continuous, watchful presence that integrates seamlessly with my remote workday.

While I work, I monitor your pet’s comfort, anxiety, and mobility in real time. My goal is to give you the freedom to recharge, knowing your family is being handled with the dignity and heart they deserve.
